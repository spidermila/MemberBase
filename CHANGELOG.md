# Changelog

All notable changes to MemberBase are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- OpenLDAP directory image (Debian `slapd`, LDAPS only, Argon2 hashes, `accesslog` change log) with the `crc` schema, the district tree and access rules enforcing Místní skupina visibility, grants and roles. (#1)
- Keycloak realm `crc`: LDAP federation (passwords written through LDAP, everything else read-only), single sign-on for MemberBase and MedCover, mandatory second factor for admin roles, passkeys, Czech login theme in MedCover colours. (#1)
- MemberBase („Evidence členů“) MVP: member list with search and filters, create/edit/move people, invitations, activate/deactivate/archive/restore, MedCover and MemberBase role assignment (also in batch), Místní skupiny, MedCover qualifications, visibility grants with expiry, change log from `accesslog`, second-factor reset, own profile. (#1)
- Jobs: grant expiry and repair of each Místní skupina's members group (`flask expire-grants`, `flask repair-members`). (#1)
- MemberBase has its own logo (also on the Keycloak login page), favicon and home-screen icon instead of MedCover's. (#1)
- The „Pozvánky“ page sends invitations to the selected people in one go. (#3)
- Bulk move of the selected people to another Místní skupina from the member list. (#4)
- MS Chair („Předseda MS“): an Admin appoints Chairs of a Místní skupina (`cn=chair` under it) in the person's role form. A Chair creates, edits, invites, activates, deactivates and archives the people of their own Místní skupina and manages their MedCover qualifications. The directory enforces the scope. Chairs cannot change people who hold a privileged role (MemberBase Admin or OS koordinátor, MedCover admin or coordinator) or another Chair; only Admins can.
- Requests („Žádosti“): a Chair asks to move one of their people to another Místní skupina, and that Místní skupina's Chair approves or rejects. A Chair or Admin asks to see named people of other Místní skupiny (typed names, a level and an optional end date), with one request per Místní skupina. Its Chair matches each name to a member (an exact match ignoring case and accents is preselected) or skips it. Deciders and requesters are emailed. MemberBase's service account carries out approved requests after re-checking them. The requester is whoever filed the request (the access rules make them name themselves); a request's status only moves forward, and nobody decides their own.
- Grants for a single person (`cn=readers-<level>` under the person entry), created by approved access requests and listed and revocable on the „Sdílení údajů“ page.
- The member list and the „Pozvánky“ page sort by any column when its header is clicked.
- The member list has a „Zrušit filtry“ button that clears the search and all filters in one click.
- The member list's role filter has „MedCover: s přístupem (jakákoli role)“, listing everyone with MedCover access (any MedCover role). (#17)
- MedCover access is a grant: everyone holding a MedCover role sees every other active holder, wherever they belong, at the contact level with their MedCover qualifications and MedCover roles (in the member list and on the person's page). District Coordinators now see a person's roles on their page too. It follows the roles, so removing the last MedCover role ends it. People without MedCover access still see only their own Místní skupina. The grant is at the contact level even where `LDAP_OWN_BRANCH_LEVEL=basic`, so MedCover users of the same Místní skupina see each other's contact details there too. The „Sdílení údajů“ page states the rule. (#17)
- Certificates („Osvědčení“): certifications, training and diplomas of a person with a title, issuer, issue date and optional expiry date, stored as `crcCertificate` entries under the person. Admins and the person's Chair add, edit and delete them on the member detail page. The „Osvědčení“ page lists every certificate the viewer may read, filterable by Místní skupina, text and validity (valid, expiring within 90 days, expired). People see their own on „Můj profil“.
- Grant level „Jméno a osvědčení“ (`records`, `cn=readers-records`): shows a person's name, Místní skupina and certificates, but not their contact details. It can be granted on „Sdílení údajů“ and requested in „Žádosti“.
- Certificate report requests („Přehled osvědčení“): an OS koordinátor (new permission `request.certificates`) asks one or more Místní skupiny for the certificates of all their current people, or of named people (typed names, matched by the Chair as in access requests), with one request per Místní skupina. The Chair brings the certificates up to date and approves. The done request then shows a live report of those people and their certificates, as far as the viewer may read them. Filed as a `crcRequest` with the new auxiliary class `crcCertificateReportRequest`. Upgrading an existing directory: add the class to the `crc` schema and replace `olcAccess` with the newly rendered `access-rules.ldif`. (#21)
- External users: the OS koordinátor (new permission `external.manage`) creates, edits, invites, activates, deactivates and archives external users and manages their MedCover qualifications and certificates, like a Chair does for a Místní skupina; the directory enforces the scope. External users get the new MedCover role `external` automatically and can hold no other role; members cannot hold it. Their invitation leads to MedCover, and MemberBase refuses their login. Upgrading an existing directory: add `cn=external,ou=roles,ou=medcover,ou=apps` and `cn=access,ou=medcover,ou=apps` (see `tree.ldif`), move any external user who holds a MemberBase role into a Místní skupina (external users can no longer log in to MemberBase), add every other existing external user to `cn=external` and take away their other roles, add the client role `external` to the `medcover` client in the running Keycloak realm, and replace `olcAccess` with the newly rendered `access-rules.ldif`. Restoring an archived external user gives the role back. (#24)

### Changed
- New people start with status `new` („Nepozvaný“) and become `invited` („Pozvaný“) only when an invitation is sent. Keycloak does not see `new` people, so they cannot log in or reset a password, and the access rules deny them everything like other non-active people. (#3)
- The `district-coordinator` role is displayed as "OS koordinátor" instead of "Okresní koordinátor".
- The navbar is a shade darker than MedCover's plain Bootstrap danger red, and its text uses the same light colour as MedCover's navbar.
- `flask repair-members` also removes archived people from app roles and `cn=chair`. A Chair who archives someone cannot change roles. It also creates `cn=chair` and `ou=requests` in Místní skupiny that predate them.
- The service account (`cn=memberbase`) may now rename people, write their `crcMemberKind` and `uid`, add children under Místní skupiny and people, change `cn=chair` membership, fill per-person readers groups and remove role members. These rights serve the requests and the members job. Since the account can already act as any person, they don't widen what it can reach, but a leaked service password could now delete people directly.
- The global „Historie změn“ shows the last 30 days (the last day after a burst of changes). It used to read the whole `accesslog`, which fails with a size-limit error once the log holds more than 5000 records.
- Fewer directory searches per page: the logged-in person takes 2 searches per request instead of 6 (roles and Chair memberships come from one search), finding people and listing Místní skupiny take one search each instead of two, batch actions look up all selected people at once, and the „Kvalifikace“ list counts holders in one search. The Keycloak service token is fetched once per request.
- Certificates are hidden from the rest of the person's Místní skupina. Only the person, their Chairs, OS koordinátor, Admins and holders of a records grant read them. The contact and extended levels do not include them.
- The service account (`cn=memberbase`) may now add (not delete or change) readers groups in Místní skupiny and under `ou=external`, so `flask repair-members` can add `cn=readers-records` where it is missing.
- Upgrading an existing directory for the MedCover grant: replace `olcAccess` with the newly rendered `access-rules.ldif` (two new rules; no schema or tree change). (#17)
- Upgrading an existing directory for certificates: with `ldapmodify` over `ldapi:///`, add `crcAttr:32`–`33` and `crcClass:12` to the schema. Replace `olcAccess` with the newly rendered `access-rules.ldif`, then run `flask repair-members`.
- Upgrading an existing directory: with `ldapmodify` over `ldapi:///`, add the new schema (`crcAttr:19`–`31`, `crcClass:9`–`11`), `olcAddContentAcl: TRUE`, the new indexes, the `refint` attributes (`crcRequestedByDn`, `crcRequestNotify`, `crcGrantTarget`) and the `unique` URI for `crcRequestId`. Replace `olcAccess` with `access-rules.ldif` rendered for the deployment, the way `entrypoint.sh` renders it; the own-Místní-skupina clause now refers to `$2`, not `$1`. Then run `flask repair-members`.
- Phones: tables (members, „Pozvánky“, „Žádosti“, „Sdílení údajů“, qualifications, change history) show each row as a labelled card instead of scrolling sideways; on touch screens and phones every button and field has one finger-sized height and the page's text size, and checkboxes are larger; the batch toolbar stays at the top while scrolling; role checkboxes on a person's page are in aligned columns (one on phones); a read-only phone number or email on a person's page can be tapped to call or write.
- Names are always surname first („Příjmení Jméno“): the create and edit forms have separate „Příjmení“ and „Jméno“ fields (both required), displayed names and sorting are built from `sn` and `givenName`, and new or edited entries store `cn` as "Surname Given". Matching typed names in access requests ignores word order.
- Roles in the member list, on a person's page and on „Můj profil“ are shown one line per app, naming the app once: „MedCover: Coordinator, Member“.
- „Žádosti“ has one „Nová žádost“ button leading to a page that lists the request types the person may file, instead of a button for access requests only. The „Žádosti“ menu item shows for anyone who may file a request. (#21)
- The email announcing a new request names what is asked for and the people it concerns (for access requests, now also the typed names). (#21)
- The access rules find everyone with MedCover access through `cn=access,ou=medcover,ou=apps`, which lists the MedCover role groups, instead of spelling every group out; with one more role the spelled-out rules overflowed slapd's 1 KB buffer for `set.expand` patterns and stopped matching. (#24)
- The change log database (`cn=accesslog`) has its own size limit of 50,000 entries instead of the global 5,000, so „Historie změn“ still loads after a busy day. Upgrading an existing directory: add `olcSizeLimit: 50000` to its database entry in `cn=config`. (#24)

### Fixed
- Moving a person to or from the external users restores their Místní skupina's `cn=members` and their roles when any step before the rename fails, not only the rename itself; before, a failure while removing their roles left them out of their Místní skupina and not moved.
- Keycloak realm: logging out of MedCover returns to MedCover's login page instead of stopping at "Invalid redirect uri" (the `medcover` client now allows `${MEDCOVER_URL}/auth/login` after logout). (#5)
- Keycloak login theme: after a password reset opened from the email in a new session, the „Účet byl aktualizován“ page links back to the application instead of ending there (a small `info.ftl` override; Keycloak itself hides the link there). The `medcover` client gets a base URL for that link. (#5)
- Every date and time in the UI is shown in Prague time; invitation, grant and request dates were shown in UTC, and an expiry date showed as the next day. An expiry date entered in a form now ends at midnight Prague time instead of UTC, i.e. one or two hours earlier.
- Clicking anywhere in a date field opens the calendar. Desktop Chrome used to open it only from the small calendar icon, so the field seemed to do nothing.
- The top bar no longer wraps or runs off the screen on desktop: it fits on one line from 1400 px and collapses into the menu button below that. MedCover's „Kvalifikace“ sits under a „MedCover“ menu, and „Můj profil“, „Změny ve verzích“ and „Odhlásit“ under the user's name.

### Security
- External users no longer read anyone through the MedCover grant (rule 10) or see who holds which MedCover role: the reader must now also be `crcMemberKind=member`. Members with MedCover access still see external users with a MedCover role. MedCover shows external users only the people of their own events from its synced copy. This narrows access. (#24)
- District Coordinators gain write access to external users (rules 3o–3u): the same attributes, statuses, additions, holdings and certificates a Chair has over a Místní skupina, and membership of the MedCover role `external`, limited by `val.regex` to external users' DNs. They cannot delete, rename or move anyone, and external users with a privileged role stay Admin-only. This widens the coordinator from read-only for `ou=external`. (#24)
- District Coordinators' read-everything clause moved out of rule 3 into a rule of its own (3n) placed after the Chair and request rules (3a–3m); Admins stay in rule 3. A coordinator therefore gets whatever those rules give anyone else before falling back to read. They may file a request (pending, in their own name, from a Místní skupina), which rule 3 used to refuse, and they still read every request. A coordinator who is also a Chair now administers their Místní skupina's people and certificates and decides its requests like any Chair. Before, rule 3 stopped them at read, although MemberBase offered those actions. Rules 4 onwards are unchanged for coordinators. (#21)
- MedCover's sync account (`cn=medcover-sync`) may now activate an invited MedCover user (someone holding a MedCover role): remove the status `invited`, add `active` and set `crcStatusChangedAt` while they are still invited, nothing else, and with no read rights beyond what it had. The directory cannot check that this happens only at a login; it trusts MedCover. A person activated this way joins their Místní skupina's `cn=members` with the nightly `flask repair-members`, so grants given to their whole Místní skupina reach them from then on. MedCover does this at the person's first MedCover login once it logs in through Keycloak, so people need not open MemberBase first. This widens the sync account from read-only to that one status change. The change log shows it as „MedCover (automaticky)“. Upgrading an existing directory: replace `olcAccess` with the newly rendered `access-rules.ldif`.
- `olcAddContentAcl` is on: whoever adds an entry needs add access to every attribute in it. Nobody may set `authzTo`/`authzFrom` outside `ou=services`.
